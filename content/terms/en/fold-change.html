Definition

Fold change is a ratio comparing a quantity with a specified reference. For positive values, define FC = treated / control and log2FC = log2(treated) − log2(control). Always name the denominator, normalization scale, and summary being compared. A ratio of arithmetic group means and the exponentiated difference of mean log signals generally estimate different quantities.

A simple example

Invented areas: treated 400 and control 100 give FC = 4 and log2FC = 2. Reversing the reference gives FC = 0.25 and log2FC = −2. A negative log2FC indicates a lower positive ratio, not negative abundance. FC = 1, equivalently log2FC = 0, means equal values on the chosen scale.

For two hypothetical paired donors, controls [1, 100] and treated values [2, 100] give a ratio of arithmetic means 51 / 50.5 ≈ 1.010. Their individual ratios are 2 and 1, whose geometric mean is sqrt(2) ≈ 1.414; the mean log2 ratio is 0.5. The donor weighting and estimand differ.

Common confusion

A model coefficient on a log2 scale can represent a condition contrast adjusted for other predictors; it need not equal a simple raw-group ratio. Read the design and contrast specification. Official limma documentation illustrates these distinctions through linear models. [1]

Fold change alone has no measure of uncertainty. A large ratio with a small or unreliable denominator can be unstable. Missing is not zero; zero denominators make ordinary ratios undefined, and adding a pseudocount changes the estimand. Relative signal change is not automatically concentration change.

Check your understanding

What does log2FC = −1 mean for treated/control?

FC = 0.5: the treated quantity is half the control quantity on the reported scale.
