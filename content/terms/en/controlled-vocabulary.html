Definition

A controlled vocabulary is a maintained collection of identified terms with agreed meanings. In MS data standards, PSI-MS supplies accessions and definitions for concepts such as instruments, acquisition parameters, processing operations, and scores. Terms may also specify value types, units, or relationships to other concepts. This supports machine-readable meaning across files and software. [1]

A label is only part of the record

Constructed example: two records both display a retention value of 12. One uses minutes and the other seconds. They represent 720 seconds and 12 seconds, respectively. A shared label or numeric value does not make the measurements equivalent. Preserve the term identity, applicable value, unit, and vocabulary version where supported.

Common confusion

A controlled term does not certify that its value was measured correctly. Structural validation asks whether a file follows its grammar; semantic validation can check that a term and unit are appropriate in context. Neither detects every scientific or sample-labeling error. Vocabulary releases evolve, so preserve the version used and check obsolete or replacement terms rather than silently substituting a similarly named entry.
