Write the contrast before running the test

Differential-abundance analysis estimates how a defined quantitative signal differs between conditions, then assesses uncertainty and multiple testing. It does not establish molecular identity or automatically imply causation. Start with a sentence such as “Estimate treated/control change in normalized protein signal across independent donors.” State the measured entity, sample denominator, reference condition, and target population. Reporting standards place design and preprocessing alongside statistical results. [1]

This Guide turns the principles in experimental design into an analysis checklist. All numbers below are invented teaching examples, not results from a biological study or recommended sample sizes.

Make the table match the independent units

Join abundances to verified metadata: biological origin, condition, pairing, preparation, batch, and injection. Technical replicates are repeated measurements of an existing unit. Summarize them with a justified rule or use a model that represents their correlation; do not count three reinjections as three independent donors. The unit of inference follows sampling and treatment assignment. [2]

For paired samples, preserve donor identifiers and estimate within-donor changes. For independent groups, use a suitable unpaired model. Additional batches or covariates may require a linear or hierarchical model. A design with controls only in batch A and treated samples only in batch B cannot separate condition from batch: adding both labels to software does not make the contrast identifiable.

Freeze a defensible preprocessing plan

Check quantitative usability, interference, and feature-to-molecule aggregation before testing. Record what each row represents; several adducts from one molecule are not independent molecular confirmations. Choose normalization from measurement evidence, not the number of significant results. Define filtering criteria and retain the tested-feature denominator. Filtering on the same apparent group difference later tested can invalidate inference.

Positive intensities are often analyzed on a log2 scale, but transformation does not guarantee normal residuals or constant variance. Inspect model diagnostics. Keep missing values visible and use an explicit missing-data strategy; an arbitrary pseudocount changes low-signal ratios. Do not generate biological p-values by replacing an entirely missing group with identical small constants.

Worked example: estimate a paired effect

The four differences have mean 1 and sample standard deviation sqrt((0.25 + 0 + 0.25 + 0) / 3) ≈ 0.408. The standard error is 0.408 / sqrt(4) ≈ 0.204. Under independent, normally distributed donor differences, an ordinary paired t-test of mean difference zero gives t ≈ 4.899, 3 degrees of freedom, and two-sided unadjusted p ≈ 0.0163.

A model-based 95% confidence interval is 1 ± 3.182 × 0.204 ≈ [0.350, 1.650]. Exponentiating gives a treated/control fold-change estimate of 2^1 = 2, with interval approximately [1.275, 3.137]. This is the ratio of geometric means, equivalently the geometric mean of paired ratios, not generally the ratio of arithmetic means. Reversing the contrast changes log2 fold change to −1 and the fold ratio to 0.5.

The calculation uses four donor differences, not eight independent samples or extra technical injections. Its interval assumes the stated model and is not adjusted for selecting this feature from many tests. Multi-feature methods can borrow information to stabilize variance estimates; that changes the uncertainty calculation and must be reported. The limma documentation explains paired designs, contrasts, and moderated statistics; its expression-data examples are not an MS performance guarantee. [3]

Define what is being tested together

A raw p-value measures how incompatible the data are with a specified null model through its test statistic. It is not the probability that the null is true. Testing many features creates many opportunities for false positives. FDR concerns the expected false-discovery proportion of a procedure, with proportion zero when nothing is rejected. [4]

Define the testing family before selecting hits. Is it all eligible proteins for one planned contrast, or all feature-by-contrast hypotheses supporting a combined claim? Two contrasts across 1,000 features can represent 2,000 hypotheses. Adjustment within each contrast does not automatically control the combined claim. Do not adjust only the already significant rows or redefine families after seeing results. Record excluded, untestable, and successfully tested rows separately.

Worked example: calculate BH-adjusted p-values

In a separate hypothetical family of five tests, sort p-values and label rank i from 1 to m = 5. Calculate m × p(i) / i, then take cumulative minima from the largest rank backward and cap at 1. Restore original row order afterward. R provides this Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment through p.adjust(p, method = "BH"). [5]

The second adjusted value falls to 0.0417 because the third ranked value is smaller after scaling. At a prespecified 0.05 threshold, three tests pass. That does not assign each a 5% error probability. BH requires valid p-values and independence or suitable positive dependence, such as positive regression dependence on a subset (PRDS); arbitrary correlation is not a blanket justification. The more conservative Benjamini–Yekutieli modification covers general dependence under its conditions. [6]

Read the result beyond its threshold

Report effect direction, magnitude, confidence interval, actual sample counts, missingness, raw p-value, adjusted p-value, method, and family size. A nonsignificant result does not establish equivalence, and a small p-value need not imply an important effect. A volcano plot cannot reveal pairing failures or confounding.

An adjusted p-value is method-specific. Storey-style q-values estimate a related FDR-based quantity and commonly estimate the null proportion; they are not automatically interchangeable with BH-adjusted p-values. Neither is an individual posterior probability of being false. [7]

Check your understanding

Does log2 fold change −2 mean negative abundance?

No. With treated/control as the direction, it means a fold ratio of 2^(−2) = 0.25, or one quarter of the reference.

Can BH correction fix p-values computed from pseudoreplicates?

No. Multiple-testing adjustment requires valid underlying tests; it cannot repair incorrect independence or a confounded design.
