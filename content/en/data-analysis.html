Keep the analysis layers distinct

A raw signal is not an identification, and an identification is not a concentration. A useful workflow preserves the evidence behind each transformation. Archive original files, sample metadata, method settings, software versions, and parameters before processing.

  1. Convert and inspect. Check scan counts, retention range, polarity, and MS levels after any conversion.
  2. Detect features. Find peaks, group isotope patterns, and connect signals over time.
  3. Align or match. Relate comparable features across runs without forcing every signal into a match.
  4. Identify or annotate. Test sequence, formula, structure, or library hypotheses.
  5. Quantify and evaluate. Integrate signals, inspect controls, and model the intended biological or chemical comparison.

Use open formats without losing provenance

mzML is a community format for mass-spectrometry spectra and associated acquisition metadata; it is not an identification-results table. HUPO-PSI maintains its specification and controlled vocabulary. [1] ProteoWizard provides tools for working with and converting MS data. [2] Conversion options such as centroiding can change the data representation, so retain originals and document the command and version.

Scores are not error rates

In peptide database searching, an experimental MS/MS spectrum is compared with candidate peptide spectra under a model of digestion, modifications, and mass tolerances. The highest score is relative to that candidate set. Expanding the search space changes the opportunity for chance matches.

Target–decoy strategies use deliberately constructed alternative sequences to estimate incorrect matches under stated assumptions. [3] A reported 1% false discovery rate (FDR) concerns an accepted collection at a specified level; it does not certify each identification as 99% correct. Peptide-spectrum match, peptide, and protein-level error rates are different. Modification-site confidence is another question.

Worked example: inspect a result table

Suppose a pipeline reports 1,000 accepted peptide-spectrum matches at an estimated 1% FDR. Under an appropriate, well-calibrated procedure, this describes a collection with an estimated false-discovery proportion around that threshold; it does not reveal which individual matches are incorrect or guarantee exactly 10 errors. Filtering to a subgroup afterward may change its error rate.

Now suppose a feature has an area of 200,000 in one sample and 100,000 in another. The observed ratio is 2. Before attributing it to concentration, examine normalization, blanks, saturation, integration, internal standards, and batch structure. Distinguish technical repeatability from biological replication.

Quality control is part of inference

Inspect mass error, retention drift, signal stability, missingness, and control-sample behavior. Missing values may represent low abundance, interference, failed identification, or acquisition choices. Replacing every missing value by zero creates an unsupported assumption. Choose filtering, normalization, and statistical models for the measurement and experimental design.

Paths into advanced computation

  • Signal processing: peak fitting, isotope deconvolution, and chromatographic alignment.
  • Identification: database search, de novo sequencing, fragmentation trees, and spectral prediction.
  • DIA analysis: trace extraction, interference modeling, and confidence estimation.
  • Machine learning: calibration and evaluation on genuinely held-out experiments.

Prevent leakage by separating related peptides, compounds, samples, or batches as appropriate to the question. Keep preprocessing fitted on training data when evaluating prediction. Report the split, candidate universe, metrics, and failure cases. A high benchmark score is meaningful only in the context of that design.

Check your understanding

Can a low FDR replace experimental quality controls?

No. Identification error control does not detect every sampling, quantification, contamination, or study-design problem. Both statistical and experimental checks are needed.
