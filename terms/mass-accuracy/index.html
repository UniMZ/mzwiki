Terminology

Mass accuracy

Separate closeness to the target from signed error, precision, and search tolerance.

TERM ENTRY · OCT 2026
ENZH

Definition

Mass accuracy concerns how closely a measured mass or m/z agrees with the value being measured. Following the metrological distinction in VIM, this entry treats accuracy as a qualitative concept and uses mass error for a numerical difference from a reference. [1] [2]

Terminology varies: an IUPAC surface-analysis glossary uses “mass accuracy” for measured mass minus reference mass. Reports often quote a ppm error or error limit under that name. Check the stated statistic and conditions rather than relying on the label alone. [3]

A simple example

Let xobs and xref be observed and reference m/z coordinates for the same ion form, charge, and isotope composition. Define the signed relative error as 10⁶ × (x_obs − x_ref) / x_ref, expressed in ppm.

Constructed measurement: xobs = 250.0010 and xref = 250.0000 give 10⁶ × 0.0010 / 250.0000 = +4 ppm. The positive sign means the observed position is higher. One such result does not establish a ±4 ppm performance limit for an instrument.

Common confusion

  • Precision describes agreement among repeated measurements under stated conditions. Tightly clustered results can all be displaced from the reference. [4]
  • Resolving power concerns peak separation or width. A narrow peak can still be incorrectly positioned.
  • Search tolerance is a chosen acceptance window; it is not a measured error, uncertainty estimate, or guarantee of correct identity.
  • Exact mass is calculated for an isotope composition; the term accurate mass refers to an experimentally determined ion mass. [5]

  • Compare width, error, and repeatability

References & further reading

  1. JCGM. International Vocabulary of Metrology, VIM3, entry 2.13: measurement accuracy.

    Accuracy is a qualitative concept and is distinguished from precision; the entry includes informative annotations.

  2. JCGM. International Vocabulary of Metrology, VIM3, entry 2.16: measurement error.

    Defines error as measured value minus reference value; basis of the explicitly signed example.

  3. IUPAC Gold Book. Mass accuracy, entry 09406.

    Surface-analysis terminology from IUPAC Recommendations 2020 uses measured mass minus reference mass; included to make differing conventions explicit.

  4. JCGM. International Vocabulary of Metrology, VIM3, entry 2.15: measurement precision.

    Agreement among replicate measurements under specified conditions; not a synonym for accuracy.

  5. IUPAC Gold Book. Accurate mass, entry 12326.

    Experimentally determined ion mass of known charge; distinguishes measured accurate mass from calculated exact mass.
