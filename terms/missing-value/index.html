Terminology

Missing value

An unavailable result whose cause and handling require explicit assumptions.

TERM ENTRY · OCT 2026
ENZH

Definition

A missing value marks a result that is unavailable for a particular variable and sample under the pipeline's reporting rules. It does not itself explain why. Distinguish missing intensity, missing identification, a measured zero, and a result rejected by a filter. mzTab-M explicitly distinguishes no data from numerical values and discusses ambiguity introduced by feature finding or alignment. [1]

A simple example

Hypothetical table: areas [100, 100, missing] have an observed-only mean of 100. Zero replacement changes the calculation to (100 + 100 + 0) / 3 ≈ 66.7. This is a changed assumption, not a newly measured third area. The observed-only mean is not automatically unbiased either.

Common confusion

Missing does not always mean absent or below detection. Signal strength, interference, acquisition, identification, and processing can contribute. Imputation estimates unobserved values; it need not improve the intended analysis. [2]

Inspect the pattern and likely causes before choosing filtering, modeling, or imputation. Preserve missingness flags and measured values, and report sensitivity to plausible alternatives. No single replacement rule is a universal default.

References & further reading

  1. HUPO-PSI and MSI. mzTab-M 2.0 metabolomics results specification.

    Official released format specification. Sections 5.2 and 5.4 distinguish samples/assays/runs and missing-data encoding; sections 6.3–6.5 distinguish molecule summaries, features, and identification evidence. A format declaration does not verify biological independence or determine the missing-data mechanism.

  2. Harris, Fondrie, Oh & Noble (2023). Evaluating Proteomics Imputation Methods with Improved Criteria. Journal of Proteome Research 22, 3427–3438.

    Original benchmark across proteomics workflows. Supports experiment- and task-dependent imputation performance and the need for an unimputed comparator. Metadata/abstract and indexed primary PMC text (PMC10949645) verified; direct PMC access encountered a bot check. No method is recommended universally.
