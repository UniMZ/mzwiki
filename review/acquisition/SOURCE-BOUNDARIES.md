# Source scope and access boundaries

Research date: 2026-10-06 UTC. This package uses official institutional/vendor documentation, IUPAC terminology, and primary research. Source text is paraphrased in original teaching prose; no third-party spectra, figures, screenshots, or extended quotations are included. Sources with partial retrieval are labeled below. No access restriction or CAPTCHA was bypassed.

## Claim-to-source register

### dia

- Source: [Gillet et al. (2012). Targeted data extraction of the MS/MS spectra generated by data-independent acquisition: a new concept for consistent and accurate proteome analysis. Molecular & Cellular Proteomics 11, O111.016717.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22261725/)
- Supports: Windowed DIA repeatedly samples precursor intervals and yields mixed fragment evidence; one window's revisit time differs from aggregate scan rate.
- Access and limits: Official PMC search-index text was readable, including acquisition description and the cycle-definition figure caption. Direct PMC open returned a browser/reCAPTCHA page; PubMed open returned a skeletal page. Used the retrieved indexed primary text, not an uninspected supplement. No CAPTCHA was completed.

### iupac

- Source: [Murray et al. (2013). Definitions of terms relating to mass spectrometry (IUPAC Recommendations 2013). Pure and Applied Chemistry 85, 1515–1609.](https://doi.org/10.1351/PAC-REC-06-04-06)
- Supports: Precursor selection and product measurement are linked MS/MS stages, not a requirement to save a fresh full MS1 before every MS2.
- Access and limits: Bibliographic identity and key are carried forward unchanged from the specified baseline. Relevant official Gold Book indexed entries were checked for MS/MS, DDA, and SRM relationships. The whole recommendations PDF was not reread. No claim of direct full-text verification is made.

### ms-order

- Source: [Thermo Fisher Scientific. Orbitrap Tribrid Series Getting Started Guide: Glossary, scan power.](https://docs.thermofisher.com/api/khub/documents/jtCzzl_yXUDJlRWIprk5vw/content)
- Supports: Common instrument MS1/MS2/MS3 notation is distinct from chronological event or cycle counts.
- Access and limits: The indexed official Getting Started Guide glossary, printed p. 145, supplied the scan-power convention. Direct PDF open failed. This is a terminology citation, not a model capability or specification claim.

### dda-selection

- Source: [Thermo Fisher Scientific. Orbitrap Tribrid Apex Small Molecule Method Editor Help: Data-dependent properties pane.](https://docs.thermofisher.com/r/Orbitrap-Tribrid-Apex-Small-Molecule-Method-Editor-Help/1563029131v2en-US2328462859)

- Access and limits: Direct official HTML readable. Top N and cycle-time-limited event counts are explicitly described. Intensity/charge/exclusion are treated as common eligibility examples, not mandatory settings for all DDA.

## Scope boundaries

- The Guide is beginner acquisition literacy, not a clinical assay, instrument operating procedure, or universal optimization recipe
- LC timing is the chosen context; the model is not automatically transferable to infusion, imaging, or other separations
- MS-level notation follows the explicitly cited instrument convention. No cycle count is interpreted as an MS exponent
- The numerical examples are invented serial schedules and coordinate exercises. No real sensitivity, ion counts, selectivity improvement, or performance ranking follows from them
- Window coverage does not establish detection, correct identification, chemical purity, or quantitative validity
- The 12 s width must use a stated convention; opportunity ratios do not impose a universal number of points across every peak
- Source product names identify documentation only. No current-product superiority, availability, benchmark, pricing, or latest-release claim appears in the articles
- Original bibliography keys remain unchanged. Only the eleven new keys in metadata/references.additions.json should be merged
