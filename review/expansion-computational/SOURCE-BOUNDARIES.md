# Source boundaries and verification

Prepared 2026-10-06 against UniMZ/mzwiki commit `ecca2688d1c33ce5485e8497b6c3e8f498376cb3`. Source metadata, the 20-Guide/44-term inventory, and relevant data-analysis/peptide-identification/small-molecule content were read through the GitHub connector at that exact commit. Local copies in `inspection/` are inspection records, not production additions.

The material is explanatory synthesis, not a review of the newest papers or a software leaderboard. Citations support specifically described methods and standards. No numerical performance claim, cutoff recommendation, or universal superiority claim is imported. All teaching numbers and logic examples are newly constructed. The cosine, correlation, percentage, and count calculations are elementary original arithmetic, not copied study results. Most practical checks are editorial recommendations derived from the information dependencies described, rather than assertions that one source experimentally established every check.

## Prediction and evaluation sources

- `comp-prosit`: Gessulat et al. 2019, DOI 10.1038/s41592-019-0426-7. Primary publisher record, abstract, and accessible supplementary descriptions verified. Supports peptide fragment-intensity/retention prediction as an example; no extrapolation to every peptide, modification, platform, or identification probability.
- `comp-massformer`: Young, Röst and Wang 2024, DOI 10.1038/s42256-024-00816-8. Publisher metadata and indexed abstract verified; direct page retrieval was intermittently unavailable. Used only for the general molecular-graph spectrum-prediction scope, not detailed architecture claims or reproduced performance results.
- `comp-multispecies`: Wen and Noble 2024, DOI 10.1038/s41597-024-04068-4. Primary publisher methods and citation information verified through indexed text. The study explicitly removes peptide overlap between species, grouping modified forms and handling isoleucine/leucine ambiguity. The Guide retains the general lesson without claiming those exact settings are mandatory in every task. These are search-derived labels, not independently established chemical ground truth.
- `comp-massspecgym`: Bushuiev et al. 2024, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks proceedings. Official proceedings PDF and author record verified. Supports explicit tasks and generalization-demanding splits. It does not establish perfect labels or unrestricted transfer to new acquisition conditions.
- `comp-similarity`: Li et al. 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41592-021-01331-z. Publisher abstract, metadata, and indexed primary full text verified. Supports defined comparisons of similarity measures. Published score cutoffs, FDR values, ranking superiority, and library-specific results are deliberately not transferred. Synthetic vector mathematics is independent of the paper.
- `comp-deeplc`: Bouwmeester et al. 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41592-021-01301-5. Publisher abstract, metadata, and accessible figure descriptions verified. Supports composition-based peptide modification representation. Does not guarantee every modification or chromatography setting is supported.
- `comp-irt`: Escher et al. 2012, PMID 22577012, DOI 10.1002/pmic.201100463. Primary publication metadata and abstract verified via PubMed. Supports the reference-scale concept and local mapping. No acquisition-window improvement is quoted.
- `comp-calibration`: Guo et al. 2017, PMLR 70, 1321–1330. Official proceedings page and paper record verified. This is general machine-learning calibration methodology; the original image/document datasets are not MS validation. The synthetic confidence-bin example is descriptive and is not a finite-sample test proving population miscalibration.
- `comp-benchmark-guidelines`: Weber et al. 2019, DOI 10.1186/s13059-019-1738-8. Original author methodological recommendations checked via publisher and author/institutional primary text. Used for general benchmark scope/fairness/reproducibility, not as experimental evidence ranking MS algorithms.
- `comp-synthetic`: Zolg et al. 2017, DOI 10.1038/nmeth.4153. Primary publisher metadata and abstract verified. Demonstrates the existence and nature of a synthetic-peptide resource. Intended synthesis identity, purity, spectrum assignment, and experimental representativeness are kept separate.
- `comp-tdc-bias`: Danilova et al. 2019, PMID 30983355, DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.8b00991. Metadata and abstract verified. Supports concern about biased scoring and correlated spectra. No inaccessible numeric result is reproduced.
- `comp-entrapment`: Wen et al. 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41592-025-02719-x. Primary publisher indexed Methods and citation information, plus PubMed metadata, verified. Supports the conservative `(D+1)/max(T,1)` expression as part of a specifically defined threshold procedure and entrapment estimator assumptions. The packet intentionally does not claim that equal database sizes alone suffice. Wrong-target/decoy behavior, score dependence, competition, selection, and reporting level all matter. The 9/1,000 count example checks arithmetic only.

## Formats and provenance sources

- Reused `mzml`: official HUPO-PSI specification landing page read directly. Spectrum/chromatogram representation, acquisition/processing metadata and validation resources are within scope. The packet makes no newest-version claim and does not say a conversion preserves all vendor metadata automatically.
- Reused `prot-mzidentml`: Vizcaíno et al. 2017, mzIdentML 1.2 standards paper, DOI 10.1074/mcp.M117.068429, checked via primary full-text index and official PSI landing page. Identification-evidence scope and spectrum references are supported. Version 1.2 is a reference for the concepts, not asserted to be latest; no generic quantification-matrix role is assigned.
- `comp-mztab`: official HUPO-PSI project documentation at https://hupo-psi.github.io/mzTab/ read directly. Original Griss et al. 2014 standard publication metadata was also verified. The tabular result-summary role does not replace the complete experimental evidence. Original mzTab and mzTab-M are not claimed to be interchangeable.
- Reused `mztab-m`: official released mzTab-M 2.0 specification read directly. Molecule-summary, feature, evidence and metadata roles are supported. The explicit 2.0 citation avoids treating a developing later specification as released.
- `comp-mscv`: Mayer et al. 2013, DOI 10.1093/database/bat009, primary PubMed/publisher metadata and indexed primary text verified. Official PSI controlled-vocabulary page and maintained PSI-MS repository also checked. Direct PMC retrieval encountered a browser check; no challenge was bypassed. The packet does not invent a CV accession or claim conformance establishes experimental validity.
- `comp-sdrf`: Dai et al. 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41467-021-26111-3, primary publisher indexed methods verified. Supports sample-to-file/channel/fraction relationships. The constructed 3-sample, 2-fraction example assumes all samples occur in each fraction; it does not infer biological replication.
- `comp-prov`: W3C PROV-DM Recommendation, 30 April 2013, read directly at https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/. Used for entities, activities, derivation and responsibility. The suggested MS manifest is editorial application, not a new standard or a mandatory workflow tool. Checksums concern byte integrity, not chemical truth.
- `comp-px-guidelines`: official ProteomeXchange guidelines PDF, version 3.0.1 dated 13 October 2019, read directly. Used only for durable distinctions among raw files, results and metadata and linking evidence for reuse. Current submission entry point also checked at https://www.proteomexchange.org/submission. Repository-specific accepted formats, embargoes, release schedules, licensing, and sensitive-data policies are deliberately not prescribed from the older guideline.
- Reused `pwiz`: existing primary ProteoWizard software citation, DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btn323, retained for the software’s role. No conversion was run and no conversion-fidelity test is claimed.
- Reused `fdr`: Elias and Gygi 2007, DOI 10.1038/nmeth1019, primary publisher record checked. Cited for the target-decoy search framework, not as a claim that one estimator applies to all later workflows.

## Scientific and editorial limits

1. Random spectrum splits, sequence splits, molecule splits, structural-family splits, instrument splits and study splits answer distinct questions. The text does not label every intended overlap leakage.
2. Unknown pretraining exposure is disclosed as a limitation; it is not declared absent.
3. Local adaptation/retention calibration is separated from transfer without local data; calibration anchors must not double as independent evaluation targets.
4. Similarity, candidate discrimination, probability calibration, retention-coordinate calibration, and quantitative analytical calibration remain separate.
5. Ground truth should be independently supported for the claim, yet standards, mixtures, consensus and simulations each have limits.
6. FDR estimates, nominal thresholds and realized error fractions differ. PSM, peptide and protein levels cannot be exchanged. Entrapment is assumption-dependent; no decoy formula is recommended universally.
7. Format conformance, semantic validation, conversion fidelity, computational repeatability and scientific correctness are distinct.
8. The publication packet does not read private research data, contact third parties, mutate the repository, run Codex, deploy a site, or claim a live-site check.
