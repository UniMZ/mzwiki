# Source boundaries

All text and teaching cases are original explanations. No published spectrum or figure is reproduced. Sources were checked through publisher pages, official records, author manuscripts, or primary-paper abstracts on 2026-10-06. Bibliographic dates refer to publication, not search crawl dates. Metadata stores stable DOI or official record links. Sources provide support for the named principle, not blanket endorsement of every surrounding teaching recommendation.

## Workflow

- `prot-fasp`: Wiśniewski et al., DOI 10.1038/nmeth.1322. Publisher abstract and metadata support detergent solubilization, filter-based exchange and digestion. The title's word “Universal” is not adopted as a universal-best-method claim.
- `prot-sp3`: Hughes et al., DOI 10.15252/msb.20145625. Primary author manuscript at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4299378/ supports a bead-based route and low-input demonstrations. We do not generalize its reported performance to every material or present recovery as complete.
- `prot-trypsin`: Olsen et al., DOI 10.1074/mcp.T400003-MCP200; primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15034119/. Supports the relationship between tryptic specificity and searching. The AKPEPTIDERAAK case separately declares its teaching rule; it is not experimental evidence that every eligible bond is cleaved or that alternative specificity can never occur.
- `prot-miape-ms`: Taylor et al., DOI 10.1038/nbt0808-860; publisher guidance. Supports recording measurement configuration and method information. Control-selection questions are pedagogical reasoning, not quoted acceptance criteria from MIAPE.
- `prot-miape-msi`: Binz et al., DOI 10.1038/nbt0808-862; publisher page and PubMed metadata. Supports transparent informatics reporting. The compact audit checklist is an original teaching synthesis, not an exhaustive or verbatim standard.
- `prot-aqua`: Gerber et al., DOI 10.1073/pnas.0832254100; author manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC165809/. Supports stable-isotope peptide internal standards. The 100/100 starting amounts with 80%/40% recoveries are constructed; they establish only the arithmetic and timing limitation stated in the example.

## Identification and inference

- `prot-sequest`: Eng et al., DOI 10.1021/pr800420s; publisher abstract and metadata. Supports spectrum-to-candidate scoring and the specific SEQUEST cross-correlation example. No universal numerical scoring threshold is inferred.
- `prot-peptideprophet`: Keller et al., DOI 10.1021/ac025747h; primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12403597/. Supports modeled assignment-confidence estimates. No claim that all posterior probabilities from all pipelines are perfectly calibrated.
- `fdr` (reused baseline key): Elias and Gygi, DOI 10.1038/nmeth1019, publisher methodological discussion. Supports target-decoy assumptions and counting choices. The 99-correct-repeats-plus-one-false case is a constructed known-truth counterexample, not a target-decoy estimator or a simulated empirical benchmark. The Guide links the existing formal FDR explanation rather than replacing it.
- `prot-proteinprophet`: Nesvizhskii et al., DOI 10.1021/ac0341261; publisher abstract and metadata. Supports probabilistic parent-protein inference and shared-peptide handling. The A/B/p/q/r case is original set logic. Parsimony is explicitly treated as a rule for explanation, not proof of absence.
- `prot-protein-reporting`: Seymour et al., DOI 10.1002/pmic.201400080; primary standards manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4384534/ and author publication record https://www.searlelab.org/publication/. Supports groups, leading proteins and representative labels. Repeated accession names are not promoted into independent protein observations.
- `prot-mzidentml`: Vizcaíno et al., DOI 10.1074/mcp.M117.068429; primary standards manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5500760/, official specification https://hupo-psi.github.io/mzIdentML/mzidentml.html and abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28515314/. Supports links among spectra, peptide evidence and database sequences and separate statistical levels. The union-of-residue-positions formula is the stated teaching definition; the 1–10/6–15 overlap is original arithmetic. Version 1.2 is a historical standards citation, not a claim about the latest version.
- `prot-proteotypic`: Mallick et al., DOI 10.1038/nbt1275; publisher abstract and metadata. Supports preferential and platform-dependent peptide observability. The term entry distinguishes empirical observability from database uniqueness and notes variation in operational usage.
- `prot-proteotypic-context`: Wilhelm et al., DOI 10.1038/nature13319; publisher Extended Data Figure 10b caption. Used narrowly for chemical-label dependence of peptide detectability. No disease, marker, exhaustive-proteome or performance-ranking claims are imported. The 9/10 versus 2/10 example is constructed.

## PTM chemistry, localization and quantity

- `prot-proforma`: LeDuc et al., DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.1c00771; primary standards manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7612572/. Supports distinct sequence forms, natural/chemical/artifactual modifications and positional ambiguity. Representation is not validation. Version 2.0 is cited for those concepts and is not labeled the latest specification.
- `prot-unimod-phospho`: official record https://www.unimod.org/modifications_view.php?editid1=21. Supports monoisotopic increment 79.966331 Da and its composition. Division by +2 is original arithmetic under stated unchanged-charge and composition assumptions. The mass shift alone is not a site or chemical-identity proof.
- `prot-enrichment`: Arribas Diez et al., DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.0c00508; primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33226818/. Supports selectivity differences and overlap among tested phosphopeptide enrichment routes. It does not establish an unbiased inventory or universally superior method. No detailed experimental protocol is prescribed.
- `prot-ascore`: Beausoleil et al., DOI 10.1038/nbt1240; publisher page and primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16964243/. Supports site-determining ions as localization evidence. ASSTK is an original theoretical sequence with only S2/S3 allowed, one modification, +1 b/y products and retained modification. The T4 residue is deliberately outside the candidate set. No experimental spectrum or guaranteed fragment yield is claimed.
- `etd` (reused baseline key): Syka et al., DOI 10.1073/pnas.0402700101. Primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15210983/ and manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC470779/ support electron-transfer fragmentation and retained labile modifications in suitable peptide ions. The text expressly avoids guarantees for all charge states, sequences or modifications and links the existing fragmentation chapter.
- `prot-ptmprophet`: Shteynberg et al., DOI 10.1021/acs.jproteome.9b00205; primary manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6898736/. Supports positional confidence modeling and false localization rates. The 100/10 conditional-error example is known-truth teaching logic, not a fitted FLR estimate. No universal localization cutoff is supplied.
- `prot-stoichiometry`: Wu et al., DOI 10.1038/nmeth.1636; primary manuscript https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3146562/. Supports distinctions among modified amount, relative change and absolute occupancy, including response-factor concerns. The 100-to-200 total-amount example is original arithmetic and does not implement the paper's experimental method.
- `prot-unimod-oxidation`: official record https://www.unimod.org/modifications_view.php?editid1=35. The entry contains residue-specific post-translational, artifact and chemical-derivative classifications. Its record-level Verified field is No. It is cited only to show that a modification label spans possible origins, not to verify any sample's chemistry or classify every oxidation event as an artifact.

## Integration and limitations

The source inspection covered the pinned repository's metadata and current fragment conventions. Some source pages expose abstracts or metadata rather than full articles; claims were limited to the exposed evidence. PMC direct opening intermittently returned a browser-check page, while indexed primary-paper text and publisher/official pages supplied the stated material. No login, CAPTCHA completion or access bypass was used.

Local checks establish consistency of this packet's arithmetic, links, anchors, citations, metadata and language pairing. They cannot establish experimental validity, perfect source completeness, publication readiness, or correct generated-site layout. Those boundaries must remain visible during later review.
