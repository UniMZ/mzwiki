Terminology

基质效应

伴随样品组分导致的响应变化，与提取损失不同。

TERM ENTRY · OCT 2026
ENZH

定义

基质效应是伴随样品组分导致的分析响应变化。在 LC-MS 中，它常指离子抑制或增强。它不同于提取中分析物损失，也不同于把独立干扰峰积分为分析物。实验可以把基质相关响应与前处理回收率区分开。[1]

简单示例

构造的匹配比较：纯溶液中的分析物产生面积 1000；向提取后的空白基质加入相同标称最终量，得到 800。基质因子为 MF = 800 / 1000 = 0.80，对应响应降低 20%。[2]如果面积为 1200，则 MF = 1.20，即增强 20%。应写明公式，因为“基质效应百分比”存在不同表述约定。

常见混淆

0.80 的因子并不表示有 20% 的分子被物理移除。高提取回收率也不能证明没有基质效应。应匹配溶剂、量、进样及基质比较，并在适用时考虑内源性分析物与干扰。

在一种来源材料中测得的基质因子，未必能代表所有样品。内标只有在其响应能够追踪相关变化的程度上，才能提供补偿。看起来干净的色谱图和狭窄的 m/z 窗口，都不能证明离子源中的抑制不存在。

在指南中应用

  • 分别计算回收率与基质因子
  • 联系基质组成与离子形成

References & further reading

  1. Matuszewski, B. K., Constanzer, M. L. & Chavez-Eng, C. M. (2003). Strategies for the assessment of matrix effect in quantitative bioanalytical methods based on HPLC-MS/MS. Analytical Chemistry 75(13), 3019–3030.

    Primary study. Author, title, volume, pages, DOI and abstract verified through PubMed PMID 12964746 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12964746/); ACS full-text retrieval was unavailable. Abstract supports distinguishing matrix effect and true recovery; exact teaching-ratio definitions are independently verified in the official Waters technical reference. No claim of full-paper inspection.

  2. Waters (2018). Bioanalytical Sample Preparation and Method Development for Therapeutic and Endogenous Peptides. Reference card 720006298.

    Official technical reference, full PDF checked. PDF pages 10–12 distinguish pre-extraction, post-extraction and neat comparisons and define recovery and matrix factor; page 2 discusses surface binding. Used for conceptual comparisons only, not peptide-specific preparation recipes or blanket container recommendations. A/B/C numbers in the wiki are original constructed examples.
