Terminology

校正 p 值

针对明确定义的多重检验集合、依赖具体方法的显著性量，并非单项发现为假的概率。

TERM ENTRY · OCT 2026
ENZH

定义

校正 p 值把某项检验结果表达在指定多重检验程序及检验族的阈值尺度上。方法很重要：Holm 以家族错误率为控制目标，Benjamini–Hochberg（BH）则在合适假设下以错误发现率为控制目标。R 的 p.adjust 实现了这些及其他程序。应报告方法与检验族，而非只写“校正后显著”。[1]

简单例子

虚构的三个检验：排序后的 p 值为 [0.01, 0.04, 0.05]。先计算 BH 缩放值：[3 × 0.01 / 1, 3 × 0.04 / 2, 3 × 0.05 / 3] = [0.03, 0.06, 0.05]。由后向前取累积最小值，得到校正值 [0.03, 0.05, 0.05]。按事先设定的 ≤0.05 规则，三项都通过。向前回取最小值这一步不可省略；只把每个 p 值乘检验数是 Bonferroni 校正，不是 BH。

常见混淆

校正 p 值为 0.03，不表示这一项发现为假的后验概率是 3%。FDR 是关于一个程序所拒绝假设集合的期望量，不是单项结果的正确概率。[2] 校正也不能修复混杂、伪重复或无效的原始 p 值。

BH 支持独立性或合适的正依赖，例如 PRDS；不能因为特征相关，就声称任意依赖都适用。Benjamini–Yekutieli 修正更保守，可在其假设下处理一般依赖。[3]

“q 值”不是没有歧义的同义词。Storey 类型的 q 值估计相关的、以最小 FDR 为尺度的显著性量，通常包含对零假设比例的估计。有些软件把 BH 校正 p 值也标作 q 值；应核对实现并报告实际方法。两者都不是局部后验错误概率。[4]

检查理解

能否只校正原始 p 值低于 0.05 的行？

不能。应对预先定义的检验族应用程序，而非只对按小 p 值筛出的集合校正。

References & further reading

  1. R stats package. p.adjust: Adjust P-values for Multiple Comparisons.

    Official documentation inspected 2026-10-07. Confirms BH/fdr, BY and family-wise error methods and family-size semantics. Both numerical BH examples are independently calculated, with backward cumulative minima and capping at one, not copied software output.

  2. Benjamini & Hochberg (1995). Controlling the False Discovery Rate: A Practical and Powerful Approach to Multiple Testing. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B 57, 289–300.

    Original definition of FDR as the expectation of the false-discovery proportion, with zero when there are no discoveries. Publisher metadata and abstract verified. The Guide illustrates the definition without prescribing the BH procedure or assuming arbitrary dependence is allowed.

  3. Benjamini & Yekutieli (2001). The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency. Annals of Statistics 29(4), 1165–1188.

    Authors’ Tel Aviv University record and full abstract inspected; official R documentation independently confirms BY general-dependence adjustment. Supports BH under suitable positive regression dependence and a distinct conservative modification for general dependence. Direct publisher page yielded no text and author PDF timed out; no claim that arbitrary feature correlation suffices for BH is made.

  4. Storey & Tibshirani (2003). Statistical significance for genomewide studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 100(16), 9440–9445.

    Primary full text inspected at PMC170937, including the q-value construction and null-proportion estimation. Used to distinguish Storey-style estimated q-values from BH-adjusted p-values and from individual posterior error probabilities. No genomewide biological findings or performance claims are transferred to MS.
