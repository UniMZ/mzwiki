# Foundational terminology integration

Integrates the supplied seven paired entries as a separate Terminology collection. The eight numbered Guides retain their source prose, metadata and order. Related-term links on Guides are derived from term metadata; term pages include related Guides and terms, references, source-edit links and matching language switches. Search exposes collection labels and a collection filter. The English index and both language routes are included in the sitemap.

The supplied source packet and reference-access limitations remain in `review/terminology/`. No additional scientific research or rewriting was performed during this engineering integration. Existing bibliography entries were preserved while merging fifteen additions. Automated arithmetic and structure checks do not constitute an independent scientific or translation review.
