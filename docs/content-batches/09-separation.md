# Separation and preparation expansion

Three new Guide pairs and six new terminology pairs, prepared against UniMZ/mzwiki commit 41af17b09a188ed7c884fcb99cfcea4efd1ef2a5.

## Reader-facing additions

- Sample preparation & recovery: selection tradeoffs, mass balance, matched pre/post/neat comparisons, internal-standard timing and boundaries
- Liquid chromatography for MS: retention, separation modes, gradient delay, peak shape/area and acquisition timing
- Blanks, carryover & contamination: control coverage, sequence history, explicit percentage denominators, interpretation and corrective-action verification
- Terms: retention time, chromatographic peak, recovery, matrix effect, carryover and blank

## Scientific checks

Examples are constructed and explicitly labeled. Equal nominal amount and matched solvent/matrix assumptions qualify recovery comparisons. Chromatographic and spectral peaks remain distinct. Cycle-interval estimates do not claim exact point counts or universal minimum sampling. Blank percentages state their denominators. ICH M10 and EPA source scope is bounded and not generalized to all MS work.

Thirteen new reference keys use the sep- prefix; four references reuse existing baseline keys. Citation numbering follows metadata order. English/Chinese fragments preserve matching section IDs, references, numbers, qualifications and language-specific internal routes.
